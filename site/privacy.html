Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Icy Weasel does not collect, store or share any information about you. The developer has no servers that receive data from the app, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. There is no account to create.

What stays on your Mac

Your history, bookmarks, open tabs and settings are saved only on your Mac, inside the app's private storage. Website data such as cookies is kept by Apple's WebKit in the same place. Private windows keep nothing once they close. You can clear history at any time from the History window, and removing the app removes all of it.

Connections the browser makes on its own

Besides the websites you visit, Icy Weasel connects to these services. None of them receive your browsing history.

  • Filter list publishers (easylist.to, ublockorigin.github.io, pgl.yoyo.org, secure.fanboy.co.nz, and filters.adtidy.org if you turn on an AdGuard list), to download blocking rules about once every three days. They see a normal download request from your network address.
  • Apple's fraudulent website warning, the same protection Safari uses. WebKit checks pages against a list of known phishing and malware sites in a way that does not reveal the full addresses you visit.
  • Your chosen search engine, only when you search from the address bar. Its own privacy policy applies to your searches. Address-bar suggestions come from your own history and bookmarks, so nothing you type is sent before you press Return.

The page assistant

The optional assistant talks only to an AI model running on your own Mac through Ollama, at a local address. Icy Weasel refuses to send page content to any other address.

Children

Icy Weasel collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome through the support page.
